**Records:** Jill 1-3, Machine 1-3, Mikey 1-3 (all same record now)

## Season Themes Emerging

### Theme 1: Momentum vs Analytics
- Jill reading momentum beats pure speed analytics (early season)
- But momentum fails on chaos (San Diego)
- Analytics fail on unpredictability (all three wrong)

### Theme 2: Dominance ≠ Destiny
- SVG had 6 of 8 road wins = should have won San Diego
- SVG WRECKED = destiny changed
- Algorithm learning: recent performance > historic dominance on risky tracks

### Theme 3: Experience Doesn't Protect
- Mikey: 39 years in racing = thought he had road courses figured
- SVG: Defending champion = thought he owned street courses
- Corey Heim: 13 years racing = hungry, nothing to lose

### Theme 4: Hunger > Credentials
- Hunger + desperation (Heim had nothing to lose)
- Beats experience + dominance (SVG had everything, got complacent?)
- Street courses reward ATTITUDE not just skill

### Theme 5: Algorithm Learning
- v3.0: Dominance and specialist skills
- San Diego: Algorithm humbled
- v3.1: Consistency + local knowledge + recent performance context
- Sonoma: Can algorithm evolve fast enough?
